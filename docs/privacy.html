🔑 DrivePass Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

The short version: we run no servers, we collect nothing, and we couldn't read your passwords even if we wanted to.

What DrivePass stores, and where

DataWhere it livesWho can read it
Your logins (sites, usernames, passwords, notes) Encrypted with AES-256-GCM into a single file (vault.enc) on your device, and — only if you connect Drive sync — in your own Google Drive. Only you, after entering your master password.
Your master password Nowhere. It is used on-device to derive the encryption key (PBKDF2-SHA256, 210,000 iterations) and is never written to disk, never transmitted, never logged. No one.
Unlocked session key Browser session memory only (chrome.storage.session); cleared when the browser closes or the vault auto-locks. Only this extension, only on your device.
Settings (auto-lock time, sync state, Google account email) Locally in your browser's extension storage. Only this extension.

What DrivePass sends over the network

  • Google Drive API (www.googleapis.com) — only if you connect Drive sync: uploads/downloads your already-encrypted vault.enc. DrivePass uses Google's narrowest Drive OAuth scope (drive.file), which grants access solely to files the extension itself created — it cannot see anything else in your Drive. Google's handling of your Drive data is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Have I Been Pwned (api.pwnedpasswords.com) — only when you explicitly click "Check my passwords" in the Security Center. Each password is hashed on your device and only the first 5 characters of the SHA-1 hash are sent (k-anonymity). The password itself, your usernames, and your sites are never transmitted and cannot be reconstructed from the prefix.

That is the complete list. There are no other network calls.

What DrivePass does NOT do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No advertising, ad networks, or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring of any user data to anyone — including data brokers or advertising platforms.
  • No collection of browsing history. The autofill content script reads login forms only to fill or offer to save credentials, entirely on-device.
  • No accounts. We don't know who you are, and we like it that way.

Affiliate disclosure

The Security Center's Recommendations tab lists security products via partner (affiliate) links. If you click one and make a purchase, DrivePass may earn a commission at no extra cost to you — this funds the free extension. These links open only when you click them; nothing is applied, injected, or tracked passively, and none of your vault data is involved in or shared with any partner.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use compliance

DrivePass's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. All permissions exist solely to provide the single disclosed purpose: managing your passwords.

Your control

  • Disconnect Google Drive at any time (Settings) — sync stops; the local vault remains yours.
  • Delete vault.enc from your Google Drive to remove all cloud data.
  • Uninstall the extension to remove all local data.
