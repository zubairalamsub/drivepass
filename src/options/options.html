PIN Quick Access

Set a PIN to quickly unlock DrivePass without typing your full master password every time.

Security tradeoff: a PIN stores your vault key on this device, protected only by the PIN. If someone copies this computer's profile they can try to guess it offline, so a PIN is weaker than your master password. Use it only on a device you trust, and choose a long, non-obvious PIN.

Privacy & your data

  • Your vault is encrypted on this device with your master password (AES-256-GCM). Google Drive only ever stores the unreadable vault.enc.
  • The optional breach check in the Security Center sends only an anonymous 5-character hash prefix to api.pwnedpasswords.com — never a password — and only when you click the check button.
  • DrivePass has no analytics, no tracking, and no ads. Nothing about you or your vault is collected, sold, or shared. Partner links in the Security Center open only when you click them.

Change master password

Reminder: there is no password reset. If you forget your master password, the vault cannot be decrypted by anyone — including you.
