🔎 Check against known data breaches

Compares your passwords against the Have I Been Pwned database of billions of leaked passwords.

What leaves your device if you run this: each password is hashed (SHA-1) locally and only the first 5 characters of the hash are sent to api.pwnedpasswords.com — never the password, never your usernames or sites. The service mathematically cannot reconstruct your password from this (k-anonymity). Nothing is sent until you click the button.
